# House style — copy to config/style.md (`clipfactory init` does this) and edit freely.
# Claude's judge and copywriter read this every time, so plain-English rules here are the fastest
# way to tune taste without touching code. Keep it short and specific; delete what you disagree with.

## What we post
- Moments a stranger gets in 2 seconds: chases, betrayals, chaos, big fails, clutch escapes, absurd RP lines.
- Funny and chaotic beat skillful. A great reaction beats a great play with no reaction.
- One clear payoff per clip. If you need to explain the setup, it's not a clip.

## What we skip
- Menus, loading, driving around with nothing happening, long monologues, reading chat or donations.
- Anything mean-spirited toward real people, and anything that only works with sound-off context missing.

## Cutting
- Start a beat before the action, never on silence. End right after the reaction lands; no lingering.
- 20–40 seconds is the sweet spot. Only go longer if every second earns it.

## Hooks (the banner on top)
- Name + action + twist: "XQC STEALS A COP CAR AND IMMEDIATELY REGRETS IT".
- Short, accurate, no fake claims, no "WAIT FOR IT", no emojis.

## Captions & copy
- Caption line reads like a friend texting you the clip. One emoji max.
- Always credit the creator.
